# Ctemz Journal

**Know why you win, and why you lose.**

Ctemz Journal is a forex trading journal built for traders who want to document their decisions, review their performance, and understand their trading process over time.

You learn from the market, books, courses, mentors, research, charts, and your own experience.

**Ctemz Journal is where you journal what you learn.**

## Why Ctemz Journal?

Trading is more than entering a position and waiting for the result.

A winning trade does not automatically mean you made a good decision. A losing trade does not automatically mean you made a bad one.

What matters is understanding the decision behind the trade.

Ctemz Journal helps you keep that record.

You can document your:

* Trade setups
* Entries and exits
* Stop losses and take profits
* Risk and position size
* Screenshots
* Trade reasoning
* Emotions and behaviour
* Mistakes
* Lessons
* Rules followed or broken
* Trading performance

Over time, those records become your personal trading history.

## The idea

The process is simple:

**Learn → Practice → Test → Trade → Journal → Review → Improve → Repeat**

You learn something.

You practice it.

You test it against historical market data.

You trade it.

You record what happened.

You review your decisions.

You identify patterns.

You improve your process.

Then you repeat.

Ctemz Journal is built around that loop.

## Features

### Trade journaling

Record the details behind every trade instead of only tracking profit and loss.

### Risk tracking

Record your position size, risk, stop loss, take profit, and other important trade information.

### Screenshots

Attach chart screenshots to preserve the market context behind your decisions.

### Performance tracking

Review your trading results and identify patterns across your trading history.

### Setup tracking

Understand which setups you trade and how they perform over time.

### Behaviour tracking

Record emotions and behaviours such as FOMO, fear, greed, hesitation, impatience, and revenge trading.

### Trade review

Look back at previous trades and evaluate whether you followed your own rules.

### Public journals

Choose what parts of your trading journey you want to share with other traders.

Your journal remains private unless you decide otherwise.

## What Ctemz Journal does not promise

Ctemz Journal does not promise profitable trades.

It does not promise a winning strategy.

It does not promise that journaling alone will make you a successful trader.

It is a record keeping and review system designed to help you understand your own trading process.

Your results depend on your knowledge, strategy, risk management, execution, discipline, market conditions, and many other factors.

## Privacy

Your trading journal is personal.

Trades, notes, screenshots, mistakes, performance, and account information can contain sensitive information about your trading activity.

Ctemz Journal is designed around private journaling first.

You decide what you want to share.

## Getting started

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Britinogn/ctemz-journal.git
cd ctemz-journal
```

Install dependencies:

```bash
npm install
```

Create your environment file:

```bash
cp .env.example .env
```

Add the required environment variables.

Start the development server:

```bash
npm run dev
```

The application will be available at:

```text
http://localhost:3000
```

## Environment variables

The application expects environment variables for services such as the API, Supabase, Cloudinary, and the public site URL.

Example:

```env
NUXT_PUBLIC_API_URL=http://localhost:8080
NUXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL=
NUXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=
NUXT_PUBLIC_CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME=
NUXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://ctemzjournal.onrender.com
```

Do not commit your `.env` file or expose private credentials.
